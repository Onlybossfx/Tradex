About TraydR

TraydR is a marketplace built for Africa and beyond — where buyers and sellers trade physical goods, digital products, freelance skills, and experiences with confidence.

Our Mission

To make commerce safe, simple, and accessible across Africa and the world. We believe every person deserves a trusted platform to buy and sell — without fear of fraud or payment failure.

Why TraydR?

Traditional marketplaces leave buyers and sellers exposed to scams. TraydR fixes this with escrow payments — your money is held safely until you confirm delivery. No more disputes over unpaid orders or undelivered goods.

Trust First
Escrow protection on every transaction. Funds only released when you're satisfied.
Built for Africa
Supports NGN, GHS, KES and local payment methods like MTN MoMo and M-Pesa.
Fast & Simple
List in minutes. Buy in seconds. No complex setup required.
Community
Verified sellers, buyer reviews, and in-app messaging keep everyone accountable.
